# AI-Cyber-Security

<div align="center">

**✨ May we walk alongside AI, not fight against it. ✨**

</div>

This repository curates key papers, articles, projects, blogs, and researchers (individuals or organizations) within the field of AI for Cybersecurity. Please note that this collection may not be exhaustive, perfectly up-to-date, entirely accurate, or comprehensive; if you have any suggestions, feel free to submit them via a Pull Request (PR) or an Issue.

The primary criterion for inclusion is that the entry has made a positive impact on the field of AI for Cybersecurity. Each entry includes a "Summary" field, which outlines the role and influence of the respective paper, article, project, blog, or researcher.

- For **Papers & Articles**, we recommend accessing the original text via the `Link` column; the `Reference` column provides additional resources for further reading.

- For **Projects**, we suggest visiting the project’s homepage and reviewing its README file; the `Reference` column offers further materials for exploration.

- For **Blogs & Researchers**, we encourage you to visit their respective homepages to learn about their contributions.
